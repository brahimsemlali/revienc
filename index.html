Market Executive Summary

The Moroccan electric scooter market has transitioned from a niche hobbyist sector to a critical component of urban micro-mobility. Driven by rising fuel costs and traffic congestion in hubs like Casablanca and Rabat, the market is seeing double-digit year-over-year growth.

Feature Priorities

Key Specification Drivers

🔋

Battery & Voltage

36V is standard, but 48V systems are gaining traction for better hill climbing in cities like Tangier. Lithium-ion 18650 cells are ubiquitous.

🛞

Tire Technology

10-inch Tubeless tires are becoming the preferred choice over standard 8.5-inch tubed tires to combat rougher road textures and reduce puncture frequency.

⚡

Motor Power

300W-350W nominal is the new baseline (up from 250W) to handle adult weight + backpacks on inclines without losing speed.

Strategic SWOT Analysis

Internal and external factors shaping the future of the market.

💪

Strengths

  • Low operating cost vs combustion mopeds.
  • High adaptability to heavy traffic in Casa/Rabat.
  • Mature spare parts availability (Xiaomi ecosystem).
⚠️

Weaknesses

  • Battery lifespan degradation in high heat.
  • Lack of dedicated service centers outside major hubs.
  • Safety concerns on shared roadways.
🚀

Opportunities

  • B2B Logistics: Last-mile delivery fleets.
  • Eco-tourism rentals in Marrakech/Agadir.
  • Integration with public transit (tramway stations).
🛡️

Threats

  • Potential strict regulation/licensing enforcement.
  • Import tariff increases on batteries.
  • Infrastructure safety limitations.
